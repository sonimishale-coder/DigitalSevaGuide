About Our Project

Digital Seva Guide is a Community Engagement Project designed to provide awareness and basic training about DigiLocker, Aadhaar, PAN services and online safety.

The website provides simple information and learning resources to help users understand and use digital services safely and confidently.

Stay Safe Online

Protect your personal information while using digital services.

  • Never share your OTP with anyone.
  • Never share your password.
  • Be careful with suspicious links.
  • Use official websites and services.
  • Do not share sensitive personal information unnecessarily.
