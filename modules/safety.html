Online Safety & Fraud Awareness

Learn how to use digital services safely, protect your personal information and recognize common online frauds.

Essential Online Safety Tips

Protect Your OTP

Never share OTPs received on your mobile phone with another person.

Use Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords and avoid using easily guessed personal information.

Beware of Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown or suspicious links received through messages, emails or social media.

Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive personal and financial information on untrusted websites or with unknown people.

Why is Online Safety Important?

Digital services make many activities easier, but users should take precautions while sharing personal information, using online accounts and making digital transactions.

Learning basic cyber safety practices can help users recognize suspicious messages, links, calls and websites.

Common Online Frauds

1. Phishing

Phishing uses fake emails, messages or websites to trick users into providing sensitive information.

2. Vishing

Vishing involves fraudulent phone calls in which a person may try to obtain sensitive information such as passwords, PINs or OTPs.

3. Smishing

Smishing uses fraudulent SMS or text messages to encourage users to click links, contact a number or provide information.

4. Fake Websites

Fraudsters may create websites that look similar to legitimate websites in order to collect personal or financial information.

5. Online Financial Fraud

Online financial fraud can involve unauthorized or fraudulent digital transactions and other deceptive activities.

6. Identity Theft

Identity theft involves the misuse of another person's identifying information.

Important Online Safety Rules

  • Never share your OTP with anyone.
  • Never share your password, PIN or CVV with strangers.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Check the website address before entering personal information.
  • Use official websites and applications.
  • Keep your mobile phone and applications updated.
  • Avoid entering sensitive information on public or unknown devices.
  • Do not trust unexpected calls or messages asking for urgent payments or personal information.

Before Clicking a Link

Before opening a link received through SMS, email, WhatsApp or social media, check the sender and the destination carefully.

  • Check the website address.
  • Look for suspicious spelling or unusual domains.
  • Do not enter passwords or financial information on suspicious websites.
  • When in doubt, open the official website directly instead of using the received link.

What to Do If You Become a Victim of Cyber Fraud?

If you experience a cyber or financial fraud, act quickly and preserve relevant information about the incident.

  1. Contact your bank or relevant financial service provider immediately if a financial transaction is involved.
  2. Keep transaction details, messages, screenshots and other relevant evidence.
  3. Report the incident through the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal when applicable.
  4. For immediate reporting of cyber financial fraud, the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal advises calling 1930.
  5. Follow the instructions provided by the concerned authorities or service provider.

Online Safety Checklist

Before using an online service, ask yourself:

  • Am I using the official website or application?
  • Am I sharing more personal information than necessary?
  • Did I verify the sender of this message or call?
  • Is the website address correct?
  • Am I being pressured to make an urgent payment?

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I share my OTP with someone?

No. Never share an OTP with another person.

What should I do if I receive a suspicious link?

Do not click the link. Verify the information through the official website or another trusted source.

What is phishing?

Phishing is a type of fraud in which deceptive messages or websites are used to obtain sensitive information.

What number can be used for immediate reporting of cyber financial fraud in India?

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal currently provides 1930 for immediate reporting of cyber financial fraud.
