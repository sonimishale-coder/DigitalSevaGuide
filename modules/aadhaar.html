Aadhaar Digital Service Guidance

Learn about Aadhaar-related digital services and understand how to access official Aadhaar services safely.

About Aadhaar

Aadhaar is a digital identity system that provides residents with an identity number. It is used for accessing various services and facilities where Aadhaar authentication or identification is applicable.

Getting Started with Aadhaar Services

Step 1: Identify Your Requirement

First identify which Aadhaar service you need, such as enrolment, update, document access or status checking.

Step 2: Use the Official Service

Use the official Aadhaar website or authorized service channel for the required service.

Step 3: Follow the Instructions

Carefully read the instructions provided by the official service before entering any information.

Step 4: Check the Service Status

Where applicable, users can use the official service to check the status of their request.

Documents and Information

The documents or information required may vary depending on the Aadhaar service being used. Users should check the current requirements on the official Aadhaar platform before starting a service.

Aadhaar Safety Tips

  • Do not share your Aadhaar-related information unnecessarily.
  • Never share OTPs with other people.
  • Do not share passwords or authentication details.
  • Be careful when clicking links received through unknown messages or emails.
  • Use official Aadhaar service channels.
  • Avoid entering personal information on unknown websites.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Aadhaar?

Aadhaar is a digital identity system that provides residents with an identity number.

Where should I access Aadhaar services?

Aadhaar services should be accessed through the official Aadhaar website or authorized service channels.

Should I share my OTP with someone helping me?

No. OTPs are confidential and should not be shared with another person.

Are all Aadhaar services available online?

The availability of a service depends on the particular service. Check the official Aadhaar platform for current information.

Always check the official Aadhaar platform for current service information, procedures and requirements.
