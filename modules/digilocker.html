DigiLocker Digital Document Services

Learn about DigiLocker and understand how digital documents can be accessed and managed through digital services.

What is DigiLocker?

DigiLocker is a digital platform that allows users to access and manage digital documents. It provides a convenient way to keep and access important documents digitally.

Why Use DigiLocker?

  • Access documents digitally.
  • Reduce dependence on physical documents.
  • Keep important digital documents accessible.
  • Share digital documents when required.
  • Access services through a digital platform.

Getting Started with DigiLocker

Step 1: Visit the Official Platform

Use the official DigiLocker website or application to access DigiLocker services.

Step 2: Create or Access Your Account

Follow the instructions provided on the official platform to create or access your account.

Step 3: Access Digital Documents

After accessing the platform, users can use the available services to access their digital documents.

Step 4: Use Documents When Required

Digital documents available through the platform can be accessed or shared according to the applicable service requirements.

DigiLocker Safety Tips

  • Never share your OTP with another person.
  • Never share your password.
  • Do not click on suspicious links.
  • Use the official DigiLocker platform.
  • Do not share confidential information unnecessarily.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DigiLocker used for?

DigiLocker is used to access and manage digital documents through a digital platform.

Can I access my documents digitally?

DigiLocker provides facilities for accessing available digital documents through its platform.

Should I share my OTP with someone helping me?

No. OTPs are confidential and should not be shared with other people.

Official DigiLocker Service

Always use the official DigiLocker platform when accessing DigiLocker services.
