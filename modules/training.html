Training & Learning Resources

This section provides simple learning resources and guidance related to DigiLocker, Aadhaar, PAN services and online safety.

The information presented here is prepared as part of the individual CEP project to promote awareness and understanding of important digital services.

Learning Objectives

Understanding Digital Services

To understand the importance and basic use of commonly used digital government services.

DigiLocker Awareness

To understand the purpose of DigiLocker and the use of digital documents.

Aadhaar Services Awareness

To provide basic information about Aadhaar-related digital services and their safe usage.

PAN Services Awareness

To understand basic information about PAN-related digital services.

Online Safety Awareness

To create awareness about safe digital practices, suspicious links, OTP protection and online fraud.

Learning Topics

DigiLocker

Learn about digital documents, accessing documents and the importance of using official digital services.

Aadhaar

Learn about basic Aadhaar services and the importance of using official service channels.

PAN

Learn about PAN-related digital services and basic precautions while using online services.

Online Safety

Learn about protecting personal information, avoiding suspicious links and keeping OTPs and passwords private.
